Add your tab (paste or upload)

Paste or upload a plain-text bass or guitar tab (lines like G|--5--7--| or e|--0--3--|). Tabs with 6 or more strings play as guitar, and 4–5 strings as bass. Change Instrument to override. Each | is a bar line. Techniques play as written: h p hammer-ons and pull-offs, / \ s slides, b bends (7b9 bends to the 9), r or (7) releases, ~ vibrato, x muted notes. Lines much longer than a normal bar play as several bars. Stacked notes play as chords and get named above the tab, and a line of chord names above a tab (Em   C) is shown over the matching bars. Use Tap on the recording to find its tempo. Lines like [Verse] become section labels.
